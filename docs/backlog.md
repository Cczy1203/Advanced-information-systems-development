## Definition of done
A backlog item is considered complete only when it meets all of the following:
- The work is implemented or documented in the shared repository.
- It has an explicit first owner and second owner.
- The item is reviewed by another team member.
- Acceptance criteria are satisfied or a reason for partial completion is recorded.
- The task status is updated in the team records and relevant evidence is preserved.
